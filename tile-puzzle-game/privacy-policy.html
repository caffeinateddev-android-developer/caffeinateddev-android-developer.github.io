This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App, including information stored locally on your device and information that may be processed by third-party advertising services used by the App.

1. Information Stored Locally by the App

The App is designed so that its primary application data is stored locally on the user's device.

We do not operate our own remote server or database for collecting or storing the local information that you enter into or create within the App.

Local application data may include information such as preferences, settings, saved progress, user-entered values, and other information required for the App's functionality.

This information is stored on your device and is not intentionally uploaded to or stored on servers operated by us.

2. Offline Use

The App's primary functionality can be used offline.

An internet connection may still be used by third-party services included in the App, particularly Google AdMob, for purposes such as requesting and displaying advertisements, processing advertising-related information, obtaining consent information, and displaying applicable privacy options.

3. Third-Party Advertising - Google AdMob

The App uses Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service provided by Google, to display advertisements.

Although the App's own primary data is stored locally, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect or process certain information when advertisements are requested, displayed, or interacted with.

Depending on factors such as your device, region, consent choices, advertising settings, and Google's SDK configuration, this information may include:

  • IP address;
  • Approximate location derived from an IP address;
  • Device identifiers and advertising identifiers;
  • App interactions and advertising interactions;
  • Diagnostic and performance information;
  • Information used for advertising and analytics; and
  • Information used for security, fraud prevention, and abuse prevention.

Information collected or processed through Google AdMob is handled by Google according to Google's own terms, privacy policies, and data retention practices.

This advertising-related information is separate from the locally stored App data described in Section 1.
